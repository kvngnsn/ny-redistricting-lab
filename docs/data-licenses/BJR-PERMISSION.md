# Benjamin J. Rosenblatt: 2022 source permission

Source: [New York 2022 Election District-Level Data](https://www.benjrosenblatt.com/new-york-2022-election-district-level-data), checked November 16, 2026.

> This data is free for anyone to use

The accompanying permission asks users to credit Benjamin J. Rosenblatt and link to the data page so that its other contributors receive credit. This project credits him and preserves the source link. The page does not incorporate the Redistricting Data Hub website terms.

The source also credits county Boards of Elections; county GIS and Planning staff; Derek Willis and OpenElections; Redistricting Data Hub for shapefile checks and corrections; the U.S. Census Bureau; USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service; and the U.S. Geological Survey. Assistance credited by an author is different from downloading data under a separate distributor's terms.

The project obtains its 2022 VTD values from the DRA v05 archive, whose [original license](DRA-ELECTION-v05.txt) identifies Rosenblatt as the 2022 source and links to this permission page. DRA's own packaging restrictions remain applicable.
