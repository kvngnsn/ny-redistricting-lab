# Data notice

These are modeled research outputs, not observed Census-block election results.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau (2020 blocks, population and VTDs); New York LATFOR (February 28, 2024 congressional assignments); Dave's Redistricting / Social Good Fund (v05 historical panel); Steve Gerontakis and John Mifflin (2008); Ryne Rohla / Decision Desk HQ (2012); VEST (2016–2020); Benjamin J. Rosenblatt (2022 and 2024); The New York Times (2024 precinct project).

“2024 Precinct-Level Election Results.” The New York Times. Accessed November 16, 2026. https://github.com/nytimes/presidential-precinct-map-2024

Modifications: historical harmonization as supplied by DRA; spatial matching and population-weighted allocation to Census blocks; district aggregation, heuristic optimization and display simplification by District Lab.
